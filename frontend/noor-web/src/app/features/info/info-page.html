The maison

Made slowly.
Worn often.

Noor is a modest atelier built on a simple belief: clothing should feel like a deep breath — beautiful, generous, and quiet enough to live in every day.

How it began

A frustration, then a collection.

Noor began in a small Hyderabad studio with a frustration familiar to many: modest wear that felt either too plain to love or too precious to wear. We wanted pieces that could go from school drop-off to work to a wedding — and feel right in every room.

So we started with fabric. Crepes that breathe. Linens washed until they soften like an old favourite. Jerseys that hold their shape through a long day. Then we cut generously, finished by hand, and kept the details quiet.

— The Noor atelier

What we hold to

Four quiet promises.

01

Fabric first

Natural fibres chosen for how they breathe, drape, and soften with wear — never for how they photograph.

02

Cut generously

Room to move, layer, and live. Our silhouettes are considered for real days, not just still photographs.

03

Made to last

Reinforced seams, checked hems, and finishes that survive the wash. Pieces meant for years, not seasons.

04

Quietly beautiful

Considered details, never decoration for its own sake. Luxury that whispers rather than announces.

The atelier

A small team, close to the cloth.

Every Noor piece passes through a small team of pattern cutters, sewers, and finishers. We work in short runs, check every hem by hand, and keep production close enough that we can walk the floor ourselves.

Our fabrics are sourced from mills we know by name. Where we can, we choose lower-impact fibres and natural dyes — and we keep packaging simple, recyclable, and free of plastic.

  • Short, considered production runs
  • Hand-checked hems and finishes
  • Recyclable, plastic-free packaging
  • Repairs and alterations on request

Delivery

Orders are prepared in our Hyderabad atelier and dispatched within one to two working days. You will receive a confirmation when your order is placed, and tracking details when it leaves us.

Method Delivery time Cost
Standard 3–5 working days ₹99 · complimentary over ₹1,999
Express 1–2 working days ₹249

We currently deliver within India only. For delivery questions, write to care@noor.store.

Payment

Cash on delivery is the only payment method available. You pay in cash when your order arrives, and we never ask for card, UPI, or netbanking details.

Online payments are intentionally disabled until a secure payment partner is integrated. Please do not send payment details by email or message.

Returns & exchanges

Unworn pieces may be returned within 7 days of delivery, with tags attached and in their original packaging. Final sale and altered pieces are not eligible.

  • Start a return from your account, or write to client care.
  • We will arrange a pickup for eligible domestic orders.
  • Refunds for cash-on-delivery orders are issued by bank transfer within 5–7 working days of receipt.
  • Exchanges are subject to stock; we are happy to help you find an alternative.
Start a return

Care & repairs

Our fabrics are chosen to soften with wear. Wash with like colours in cold water, line dry in shade, and avoid tumble drying to preserve drape and finish. Each piece arrives with a care card specific to its cloth.

If a seam or finish needs attention, write to us. We keep a small repairs bench and will do what we can to give your piece a longer life.

Questions

Client care is available Monday–Saturday, 10am–6pm IST, at care@noor.store or +91 98490 14820.

Fit & measurements

Size guide.

Our pieces are cut generously for graceful movement. Use the guide below, and write to us if you would like a personal fit recommendation.

How to measure

  1. 01
    Bust

    Measure around the fullest part, keeping the tape level and relaxed.

  2. 02
    Waist

    Measure around your natural waist, without pulling the tape tight.

  3. 03
    Hip

    Measure around the fullest part of your hips, feet together.

  4. 04
    Length

    Measure from the shoulder to where you would like the hem to fall.
